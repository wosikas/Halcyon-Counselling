Understanding Life's Challenges

Life brings moments of joy, but also times of uncertainty, stress, sadness, and heartache. These challenges can sometimes feel overwhelming. Whether you’re navigating relationship struggles, coping with loss, adjusting to life transitions, or facing the unique challenges of infertility, you don’t have to face these things alone.

When Anxiety Holds You Back

Anxiety often keeps us stuck in an endless loop, where asking for help can feel just too difficult. Taking that first step can feel daunting, but it’s also an act of courage. I'm so glad you've found your way here.

A Safe Space To Move Forward

With me, you’ll find a safe, supportive, and confidential space where you can share your thoughts and feelings openly. Together, we’ll explore your triggers, understand the root of your struggles, and find effective ways to move forward.

How I Can Help

In addition to working with a wide range of concerns, including anxiety, depression, grief, and loss, I have specialist expertise and experience in the following areas.

Fertility Counselling

Supporting you at every stage of your conception journey; from first hopes, through treatments and beyond.

Perinatal Counselling

Ensuring your wellbeing through the changes of pregnancy, postnatal life and the transition to parenthood.

Couples Counselling

Providing a safe space for open communication to explore difficulties, resolve conflict and consider the best way forward.

Life Transitions

Helping you navigate your way through life changes, such as redundancy, menopause, parenthood, loss and ageing.
