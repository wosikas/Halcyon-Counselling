# Competitor profile: Coffee Lady (named by the user as a likely competitor)

Source: https://coffeelady.uk/ (homepage, fetched 1 Nov 2026). All claims below are the company's own website claims.

## Takeaway
Coffee Lady is a founder-led independent (Jan Revill) based in Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire. It sells, leases, installs, services and repairs multi-brand commercial coffee machines for offices, co-working spaces and hospitality. It positions on personal service and expertise. It publishes no prices, contract terms or response times, and it does not offer coffee/bean supply, rental or trials on the homepage. This is close to the business model the user is considering, and it is a benchmark for what an independent B2B coffee machine business looks like today.

## Cited findings (https://coffeelady.uk/)
- Location: Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire; claims "UK-wide Nationwide coverage".
- Scale claims: "25+ Years of experience"; 500+ businesses served; 500+ machines serviced annually.
- Customers: offices, cafés, hotels, restaurants, hospitality venues, co-working spaces, start-ups/growing businesses (leasing).
- Services: machine sales with "full installation and team training included"; leasing with "no large upfront costs"; fast repairs; planned servicing; maintenance (cleaning, descaling) for "one fixed, predictable cost".
- Categories: bean-to-cup, barista/traditional espresso, coffee vending, commercial brewers, water coolers and water taps.
- Brands: Bestir, Bravilor Bonamat, Fracino, Franke, Iberital, Jura, La Pavoni (Smeg), Lavazza, WMF, AA First Water Coolers & Boilers, Dalla Corte. The site image metadata references a Franke Mytico Due.
- Trust signals: "5.0★ Google-rated service"; "FCA-Authorised Finance"; qualified installers/engineers; authorised brand dealer and service partner status; testimonial from an office manager naming Jan.
- Marketing: "Get a Free Quote", "View Our Machines", "Speak to an Expert"; content marketing through buying guides written by Jan Revill (e.g. office coffee machine costs, 2026 comparisons).
- Contact: 01844 391222; jan@coffeelady.uk.

## Inferences
- Gaps against the "no lock-in, transparent" positioning in the main report: no published prices, terms, response-time SLAs, free trial or coffee supply. A new entrant could differentiate on transparent online pricing, trials, rolling contracts and an own-label bean/consumables subscription.
- Coffee Lady's FCA-authorised finance and dealer/service-partner status set the bar a newcomer must match, or rent through partners (lease brokers, IAR principal, subcontracted engineers).
- Founder-centred brand (Jan Revill) and 25+ years' trading: possible partner (subcontracted installs and servicing in Bucks/Oxon/Herts/Beds), or a future succession/customer-book acquisition candidate. Ownership intentions are unknown and unresearched.
- An earlier research note also mentions "Coffee Lady" as a UK-wide engineer network that could take subcontracted install and service work. It is unconfirmed whether that is the same business.

## Gaps
- Companies House filings (turnover, staff), Google review count, contract terms and prices, service SLAs, and whether they supply coffee/consumables (other pages of the site were not reviewed).
