# Supply side for a UK coffee machine and coffee business: wholesalers, distributors, trade terms, refurbished supply and compliance (2025–2026)

Research method note: about 20 web searches and fetches, run in November 2026. Several primary sites blocked automated fetching, including trade-tariff.service.gov.uk (HTTP 403). Distributors' trade terms and discount levels are almost never published. Where no number could be sourced, it is listed under Gaps, not estimated. Items marked "(statute; not re-fetched this session)" cite the primary legislation URL from established knowledge and should be checked against the live text.

## 1. UK distributors and wholesalers of home and prosumer machines, and trade account terms

### Takeaway
The prosumer brands (Rocket, Lelit, ECM, Profitec, Ascaso, La Spaziale, Gaggia) each reach the UK through one official importer or a small set of "authorised dealers" that are often also retailers or roasters. That makes a new entrant both a customer of these distributors and a competitor to them. No source publishes trade discount levels or account requirements. These have to be obtained by direct application. Independent electrical retailers can get mass-market brand terms through buying groups such as Euronics/CIH and Sirius.

### Cited Findings
- **Rocket Espresso:** Rocket Espresso UK, formerly Pro Espresso, is listed on Awin as the brand's official UK distributor and has an affiliate programme running for more than 5 years — [Awin merchant profile](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/38418)
- A Coffee Forums advertiser describes itself as an official UK Rocket dealer offering free "white glove" delivery and setup. This shows dealers compete on service rather than price — [Coffee Forums](https://www.coffeeforums.co.uk/threads/introducing-free-white-glove-rocket-espresso-machine-delivery-and-setup-across-the-uk.61304/)
- REPA is the authorised distributor of original Rocket spare parts in the UK — [REPA](https://repagroup.com/?p=230687)
- **Lelit and ECM:** Bella Barista, a Wellingborough roaster and retailer, says it is an official UK distributor for Lelit and ECM's premium UK partner. It serves trade, retail and individual customers and also sells Profitec — [Bella Barista news](https://news.bellabarista.co.uk/portfolio/rocket-appartamento-and-fausto/); [Bella Barista heat-exchanger collection](https://bellabarista.co.uk/collections/heat-exchanger)
- **Profitec:** no named UK distributor was found. Profitec is made by the same Heidelberg group as ECM, and Bella Barista sells it — [Bella Barista blog](https://bellabarista.co.uk/blogs/coffee-machines?page=4)
- **Ascaso:** Ascaso set up Ascaso UK, based in Ware, Hertfordshire, as its sole official principal. Fairfax previously imported the brand. The article is undated — [Housewares Live](https://housewareslive.net/coffee-machine-company-ascaso-sets-up-uk-office/)
- **Gaggia:** Gaggia (UK) Ltd went into administration in 2009, and Watermark Coffee Technology (Dublin) became the commercial importer — [The Caterer](https://www.thecaterer.com/articles/330071/rich-coffee-and-caffe-shop-tackle-uk-gaggia-supply-hole). Gaggia has since relaunched into UK foodservice — [The Caterer](https://thecaterer.com/products/gaggia-relaunches-into-uk-foodservice-market). - **La Spaziale:** no single UK distributor was identified. Several retailers say they are "approved La Spaziale partners", including Clumsy Goat and Café du Monde — [Clumsy Goat](https://clumsygoat.co.uk/collections/la-spaziale/products/la-spaziale-s2-commercial-espresso-machines-1-2-3-group-models-available); [Café du Monde](https://www.cafedumonde.co.uk/la-spaziale-coffee-machines/)
- **Fracino (UK manufacturer):** Fracino has a 50,000 sq ft factory in Birmingham. About 90% of each machine's components are fabricated and assembled there, and it has been a Made in Britain member since 2017 — [Made in Britain case study](https://www.madeinbritain.org/about/case-studies/fracino). Its sales and service teams support a "growing customer and distributor network nationally and internationally" — [Made in Britain news](https://www.madeinbritain.org/news/service-with-a-smile-as-fracino-further-bolsters-customer-support). It exports to more than 70 countries — [Manufacturing Today](https://manufacturing-today.com/profiles/fracino). No published dealer terms were found.
- **Euronics / CIH (Combined Independents Holdings):** CIH is the UK arm of Euronics, with about 450 members and 600+ stores. In April 2025 it lowered its contribution threshold and introduced an associate membership as a stepping stone — [KBB Review](https://www.kbbreview.com/72265/news/cih-lowers-contribution-threshold-to-attract-new-members/); [Euronics press release, Hughes joins CIH](https://www.euronics.co.uk/hughes-electrical-new-cih-member-press-release)
- **Sirius Buying Group** is member-owned, serves independent electrical and kitchen retailers, partners with Bira, and added seven Approved Members in 2026 — [ERT Online](https://ertonline.co.uk/news/sirius-welcomes-a-host-of-new-members-in-2026/); [ERT Online, Sirius and Bira](https://ertonline.co.uk/news/sirius-and-bira-partnership/)
- **Dealer margins:** no UK source for espresso-machine dealer margins was found. A MAP explainer uses a hypothetical example in which a MAP 20% below MSRP protects a 20% retailer margin. It also notes products are often advertised below MAP, sometimes at or below dealer cost — [Pricefy](https://www.pricefy.io/articles/what-is-map); [FER magazine](https://www.fermag.com/articles/796-protecting-dealer-profit/)

### Inferences
- Many prosumer distributors (Bella Barista, Rocket Espresso UK) also sell direct to consumers. A new online retailer would be buying from its competitors, and brands may restrict new dealers to protect existing ones, as Rocket's US "support our dealer network" stance suggests. Expect to need a showroom or service capability, a minimum opening order and MAP compliance.
- Joining a buying group (CIH associate membership or Sirius) is the realistic route to mass-market brands such as De'Longhi, Sage and Melitta for a small independent.

### Gaps
- No published trade discount percentages, opening-order minimums or credit terms for any named distributor. Request trade price lists directly.
- Sage (Breville) UK trade or B2B account terms: no source found.
- Nisbets, CaterKwik and Buying Solutions trade pricing structures: not researched in depth. CaterKwik appeared only as a source of engineer callout rates.
- CIH and Sirius membership fees and rebate levels are not published.

## 2. Commercial machine suppliers, remanufacturers, and refurbished or ex-lease supply

### Takeaway
A mature UK market exists for refurbished and ex-lease commercial espresso machines. Lease companies such as Liquidline refurbish returned machines in-house and resell them. Smaller specialists (Clumsy Goat, Logic Vending, Advance Coffee Solutions, Espresso Services, Café du Monde) sell reconditioned La Spaziale, Iberital, Fracino and Expobar units. No source gave trade buy-in prices or refurbishment margins.

### Cited Findings
- Liquidline leases new machines on fixed monthly terms of 3 to 5 years. Machines returned at lease end are refurbished in its workshop and resold — [Liquidline refurbished machines](https://www.liquidline.co.uk/refurbished-commercial-coffee-machines)
- Clumsy Goat is an approved UK Iberital partner selling refurbished Iberital IB7 and La Spaziale S40 machines that its engineers inspect, service and test — [Clumsy Goat IB7](https://clumsygoat.co.uk/products/iberital-ib7-commercial-espresso-machines-refurbished); [Clumsy Goat S40](https://clumsygoat.co.uk/products/la-spaziale-s40-suprema-espresso-machines-2-group-refurbished)
- Logic Vending sells used and refurbished two-group machines, including Fracino and Expobar, to buy, lease or rent — [Logic Vending](https://logicvending.co.uk/commercial-coffee-machines/refurbished-coffee-machines)
- Advance Coffee Solutions specialises in used and refurbished commercial espresso machines and runs a call-out service — [Advance Coffee Solutions](https://advancecoffeesolutions.yolasite.com/second-hand-machines.php)
- Café du Monde sells refurbished commercial machines to buy, lease or rent — [Café du Monde](https://cafedumonde.co.uk/refurbished-coffee-machines)
- Espresso Services is listed as a supplier of new and reconditioned commercial espresso machines — [FreeIndex directory](https://www.freeindex.co.uk/categories/entertainment_and_lifestyle/food_and_drink/coffee_machines/)

### Inferences
- The best source of ex-lease stock is probably the large lease and office-coffee operators: Liquidline, Coffeetec-type operators and vending companies. Buying direct from them in bulk, before they refurbish, is a possible supply route, but this is not evidenced.
- Commercial refurbishment needs qualified engineers. Boilers are pressure equipment and machines may be gas or three-phase, so labour is the main cost and the main quality risk.

### Gaps
- No published trade or wholesale prices for unrefurbished ex-lease machines, and no gross margins on refurbished units.
- Coffeetec, Coffee Machine Sales and Prima Coffee were not specifically researched. Their terms are unknown.

## 3. Refurbished and returned consumer machines (B-stock, liquidation, manufacturer refurbishment)

### Takeaway
Graded consumer returns from Sage, De'Longhi and Jura are resold through Back Market (UK, minimum 1-year warranty), eBay (often "C grade" or "spares or repairs") and overseas retailer "second chance" schemes. No reliable data was found on liquidators' grading, pallet pricing or margins (Jobalots, Wholesale Clearance UK, Lotify).

### Cited Findings
- Back Market UK lists refurbished Sage and Jura machines with a minimum one-year warranty, which sets the consumer expectation for refurb warranties — [Back Market UK, Jura](https://backmarket.co.uk/en-gb/l/jura-coffee-machine/9f3d7f64-5684-4d55-953e-cdd786b10787)
- eBay sellers list De'Longhi and Sage bean-to-cup machines as untested or "spares or repairs only". This shows a meaningful share of returns are faulty — [eBay UK listing](https://www.ebay.co.uk/itm/145504757679); [eBay DE listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/306638746775)
- Coolblue (Netherlands) runs a "Second Chance" scheme for visibly or lightly damaged De'Longhi machines at reduced prices. This is the retailer B-stock model, but it is not a UK source — [Coolblue](https://www.coolblue.nl/en/coffee-machines/coffee-machines-with-beans/second-chance)

### Inferences
- Bean-to-cup returns carry real quality risk: scale, milk-system contamination, grinder and brew-unit faults. Food-contact hygiene (see section 6) makes deep cleaning and parts replacement mandatory, which eats into margin.
- A refurbisher who resells is likely to be treated as the trader under the Consumer Rights Act, and may count as a "producer" for product-safety purposes if it substantially modifies the product. A refurb business must therefore carry its own warranty provision.

### Gaps
- Jobalots, Wholesale Clearance UK and Lotify: no source found on coffee-machine pallet prices, grading or return rates.
- Manufacturer refurbishment programmes (Sage refurbished, De'Longhi outlet): not confirmed.
- No source for typical refurb margins.

## 4. Chinese and OEM private-label machines

### Takeaway
Alibaba carries many 15–20 bar vibration-pump (Ulka-type) and E61-style machines with private-label options. Listed prices run from about US$160 to US$659 per unit at MOQs of 20–50 units for a custom logo and 500 units for custom packaging. Listings are inconsistent and need independent verification, especially of the electrical-safety certification needed for the UK market.

### Cited Findings
- A 15-bar vibration-pump E61 machine was listed at US$349–659 with a 20-piece MOQ — [Alibaba listing](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/_1601072146283.html)
- Another 15-bar listing shows a customised-logo MOQ of 50, a customised-packaging MOQ of 500 and a sample price of US$630 — [Alibaba listing](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/_62379300080.html)
- A 15-bar Ulka-pump machine was priced at US$170 for 20 pieces and US$160 for 40 pieces, on FOB terms — [Freshdi](https://freshdi.com/product/Espresso-and-Cappuccino-Coffee-Machine-Stainless-Steel-Fully-Automatic-Intellige)
- Alibaba's own seller guide says the 15, 19 and 20 bar figures are pump maximum or stall pressure, not brewing pressure. Listings therefore overstate performance — [Alibaba seller blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/coffee-machines/pressure-rating-9bar-15bar-guide-alibaba-b2b)
- UK commodity code 8516710000 covers coffee or tea makers (electro-thermic). HMRC issued an Advance Tariff Ruling placing a USB portable espresso maker under this code, valid 23 Apr 2024 to 22 Apr 2027 — [HMRC Advance Tariff Ruling 600010352](https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/search-for-advance-tariff-rulings/ruling/600010352)
- The third-country import duty on 8516710000 is listed at 2.00%, plus 20% import VAT. This comes from an aggregator because the official tariff site blocked the fetch, so verify on trade-tariff.service.gov.uk — [Treayo](https://treayo.com/en/hs/uk/8516710000)

### Inferences
- Indicative landed cost per unit: FOB price, plus freight and insurance, plus about 2% duty, plus 20% import VAT (recoverable if VAT-registered), plus UKCA/CE test reports, plus WEEE and packaging obligations. The importer is the legal "manufacturer" or "importer" under the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations and carries product liability.
- An importer must hold the technical file and Declaration of Conformity, and its name and address must be on the product. Many Alibaba "CE" certificates are self-issued or belong to a different model. Independent LVD/EMC testing, budgeted separately, is prudent.
- Brand-owned Chinese ecosystems (Xiaomi, Bear) were not shown to offer UK private label.

### Gaps
- Canton Fair OEMs, Bear and Xiaomi-ecosystem private-label terms: no sources found.
- Cost of independent LVD/EMC testing per model: not found.
- Duty rates from preferential origins (Italy and EU under the TCA = 0% if origin rules are met; China = third-country rate) were not verified on the official tariff.

## 6. Compliance obligations and hidden costs

### Takeaway
Selling mains-powered coffee machines in GB requires conformity with the Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016. CE marking remains accepted indefinitely alongside UKCA. Every seller must also meet WEEE retailer take-back duties. The Valpak Distributor Take-back Scheme (DTS) costs £112.50 for three years for small sellers, or a per-unit fee with a £112.50 minimum for online sellers. Importers or brand owners are also WEEE "producers". Other obligations include packaging EPR above £1m turnover and 25 t, VAT registration above £90k, consumer-law returns and warranty duties, and EU GPSR (EU Responsible Person) for EU and Northern Ireland sales.

### Cited Findings
**Product safety and marking**
- Legislation in force from 1 October 2024 continues recognition of CE marking in GB. Businesses can choose UKCA or CE, and electrical equipment is listed as "UKCA or CE" under DBT — [GOV.UK product marking guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/current-approaches-to-product-marking/product-regulations-and-current-approaches-to-product-marking-for-the-gb-market); [GOV.UK EESR 2016 statutory guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electrical-equipment-safety-regulations-2016)
- Commentators describe CE recognition as indefinite, but note it is a policy position rather than a guarantee — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/uk-government-extends-the-recognition-of-the-ce-mark-in-great-britain-indefinitely); [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/deadline-new-ukca-product-marking-extended-indefinitely)
- The Product Regulation and Metrology Act 2025 received Royal Assent on 21 July 2025. It is enabling legislation that lets ministers make product-safety rules, including for online marketplaces, and the government has said it will align with the EU where sensible — [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/20); [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2024/12/02/general-product-safety-regulation-applies-from-13-december-102jq01); [NI Assembly Research](https://www.assemblyresearchmatters.org/2025/09/30/the-product-regulation-and-metrology-act-2025-what-it-means-for-northern-ireland/)
- EU GPSR (Regulation 2023/988) has applied since 13 December 2024 in the EU and Northern Ireland, not GB. Products need an EU- or NI-based responsible economic operator. GPSR also places duties on online marketplaces — [GOV.UK GPSR NI guidance (updated 4 Nov 2025)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/general-product-safety-regulations-northern-ireland/eu-regulation-2023988-on-general-product-safety-detailed-guidance); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/resources/product-risk-radar-articles/general-product-safety-regulation); [Pirate Ship explainer](https://support.pirateship.com/en/articles/10228339-what-is-the-gpsr-for-products-going-to-the-eu-and-northern-ireland)

**WEEE**
- Distributors (retailers) must either offer in-store one-for-one, like-for-like take-back or join the DTS. Stores with £100k+ EEE sales cannot use the DTS and must offer in-store take-back. Online and distance sellers of any size can join. Phase 7 runs from 1 Jan 2024 to 31 Dec 2026 — [Valpak DTS](https://dts.valpak.co.uk/); [ERP UK](https://erp-recycling.org/uk/news-and-events/2024/07/new-phase-of-the-weee-distributor-takeback-scheme/)
- Phase 7 DTS fees: Band B (small retailer, under £100k sales) pays £112.50 for 3 years. Band A (online or distance sellers) pays per unit sold, with "all other EEE" (which includes coffee machines) at 0.028 per unit (currency not stated on the page; presumably £) and a £112.50 minimum. Retailers with sales areas of 400 m² or more pay a 25% surcharge — [Valpak DTS Fees](https://dts.valpak.co.uk/DTS/Fees). Bira quotes a range of £60 to £190+ + VAT, which is probably older — [Bira](https://bira.co.uk/resources/selling-electrical-goods-join-the-distributor-take-back-scheme/)
- Producers (importers, own-brand sellers) placing under 5 tonnes of EEE a year can register directly as a small producer through the WEEE online service, used since January 2025. Above that they must join a producer compliance scheme. The next small-producer registration deadline is 31 Jan 2027 — [GOV.UK EEE producer responsibility](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/electrical-and-electronic-equipment-eee-producer-responsibility); [GOV.UK WEEE data service levels](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/producer-responsibility-regulatory-services-weee-data-service-levels/weee-data-service-levels-2024-producer-responsibility-regulatory-services). The historic small-producer fee was £30 a year (2014). The current fee was not verified — [IEMA](https://iema.net/articles/weee-2014-plans-confirmed)

**Packaging EPR**
- Small producers (£1m+ turnover and over 25 t of packaging) must register and report. Large producers (£2m+ and over 50 t) also pay waste-management fees. 2025–26 base fees per tonne: plastic £423, paper and card £196, glass £192, aluminium £266, fibre composite £461. Fees are modulated by recyclability from 2026–27 — [PKF](https://www.pkfscs.co.uk/gov-releases-epr-base-rates-2025/); [ERP UK 2026 guide](https://erp-recycling.org/uk/news-and-events/2026/04/uk-packaging-epr-compliance-guide-for-2026/)
- Environment Agency registration charges for 2026: small producer registering directly £1,303 (up from £1,216), or £696 through a compliance scheme (up from £631) — [Let's Recycle](https://www.letsrecycle.com/news/provisional-pepr-charges-for-2026-published). Sources conflict on small-producer reporting deadlines (April versus 1 Oct 2026) — [ERP UK small producers](https://erp-recycling.org/uk/news-and-events/2026/07/packaging-epr-for-small-producers-2026-reporting-obligations/)

**VAT and duty**
- The VAT registration threshold is £90,000 of taxable turnover over any rolling 12 months, from 1 April 2024. Non-established taxable persons, including overseas sellers, have no threshold — [GOV.UK VAT Notice 700/11 supplement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-notice-70011-cancelling-your-registration/vat-notice-70011-supplement)
- Import duty on coffee machines (8516710000) is about 2% third-country, plus 20% import VAT. See section 4 — [Treayo](https://treayo.com/en/hs/uk/8516710000)

**Consumer law (statute; not re-fetched this session)**
- Consumer Rights Act 2015: goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. Consumers have a 30-day short-term right to reject, then repair or replacement, then price reduction or final rejection. Faults appearing within 6 months are presumed present at delivery. This applies equally to refurbished goods, judged against their description and price — [legislation.gov.uk CRA 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/contents)
- Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013: distance sales carry a 14-day cancellation period from delivery. The refund, including standard outbound delivery, is due within 14 days of receiving the goods back or evidence of return. The trader may deduct for diminished value from handling beyond what is needed to establish the goods' nature. Return costs fall on the consumer only if they were told in advance — [legislation.gov.uk SI 2013/3134](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/contents)
- Food-contact materials: Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, as retained in GB, requires materials in contact with food, including brew paths, milk systems and jugs, not to transfer constituents that endanger health. Importers of own-brand machines should obtain migration test evidence — [legislation.gov.uk Reg 1935/2004](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2004/1935/contents)

### Inferences
- Hidden fixed compliance costs for a small online seller are modest: DTS from £112.50 for 3 years, WEEE small-producer registration (if importing), product liability insurance, and test reports for own-brand imports. Packaging EPR only applies above £1m turnover and 25 t.
- The larger hidden cost is consumer returns. A 14-day no-reason cancellation on espresso machines produces used-but-returnable B-stock, which needs a cleaning and refurb pipeline. Hygiene also limits what can be resold as new.
- Marketplace sellers (Amazon, eBay) will face platform-enforced GPSR and EU Responsible Person requirements for EU and NI listings, and possibly future UK marketplace rules under the PRaM Act 2025.

### Gaps
- Product liability insurance premiums for an appliance retailer or importer: not researched.
- Exact current WEEE small-producer fee, and the currency and unit of the DTS Band A per-unit rate: not confirmed.
- Whether DTS Phase 8 (from 1 Jan 2027) changes fees: not found.
- Secondary legislation under the PRaM Act on online marketplaces in force as of late 2026: not confirmed.

## 7. After-sales service: repair networks, callout economics and warranty provisions

### Takeaway
No coffee-specific UK published callout tariff was found. Adjacent catering-equipment benchmarks put a callout at about £120–£195 including the first hour, plus about £75–£80 per extra hour. Brand-authorised parts channels exist (REPA for Rocket), and Fracino runs its own national sales and service teams.

### Cited Findings
- Industrial Warewashers' catering engineer network charges a £149.99 initial callout including the first hour, with further labour at £75/hour — [Industrial Warewashers](https://industrial-warewashers.com/catering-equipment-engineers)
- CaterKwik's engineer callout sheet (rev. May 2026) lists standard callouts of about £186–£195 in Cumbria, including the first 60 minutes — [CaterKwik PDF](https://www.caterkwik.co.uk/pdf/doc219EngineerCallOutRev10.114.05.2026.pdf)
- A non-coffee maintenance firm's 2026 rates are £120 + VAT for the first hour and £80 + VAT for each additional hour on weekdays — [Advanced Maintenance](https://www.advancedmaintenance.co.uk/post/our-2026-call-out-service-rates)
- Advance Coffee Solutions offers a coffee-machine call-out service, with rates not captured — [Advance Coffee Solutions](https://advancecoffeesolutions.yolasite.com/call-out-service.php)
- Fracino has expanded its in-house customer service and support teams — [Made in Britain](https://www.madeinbritain.org/news/service-with-a-smile-as-fracino-further-bolsters-customer-support)
- REPA is the authorised UK distributor of original Rocket spare parts — [REPA](https://repagroup.com/?p=230687)

### Inferences
- Commercial: a single on-site callout (about £150–£200) is a large share of the margin on a refurbished machine. Bundling a service contract or limiting warranties to return-to-base is common practice and would help.
- Home and prosumer: return-to-base courier repair is likely cheaper than engineer visits. A warranty provision should be modelled per unit from expected failure rates, which were not found.

- Coffee-specific engineer callout rates and annual service contract prices (UK): not found.
- Warranty claim rates and typical warranty provision (% of sales) for espresso and bean-to-cup machines: not found.
- Economics of third-party engineer networks (subcontract rates paid by distributors): not found.
