# UK Coffee Machine Market Size and Customer Behaviour (Home and Small-Business Buyers), 2025–2026

Research note, compiled 1 Nov 2026. About 20 searches and fetches. Most high-quality UK data (Allegra/World Coffee Portal, Mintel, GfK/NIQ, Statista) is paywalled. Figures below come from free summaries, press releases and secondary pages, and each is labelled with its year. Vendor and brand-commissioned surveys are flagged as such.

## 1. How big is the UK coffee machine market, how is it growing, and how does it split by type and price?

### Takeaway
The most credible UK figure is Allegra/World Coffee Portal's: consumer coffee machine sales of about **£163m and about 1.76m units a year (2023 report)**, forecast to reach about **£183m and 1.83m units by 2026**. That gives an implied blended average selling price of only about £90–£100, because cheap pod and filter units dominate volume. Pods were about half of sales by value (2023). Espresso (which includes bean-to-cup in WCP's classification, as far as can be told) was rising toward 30%. Commissioned global-research-firm estimates for the UK range widely, from about US$310m to US$900m, and should be treated as low-quality.

### Cited Findings
- UK consumer coffee machine sales: £123.2m (2019), £157.2m (2022), and £162.8m in the latest 12 months (+3.5%), per Allegra Coffee At Home UK 2023. Forecasts are £169.5m in 2024 (+4.1%) and £183.2m in 2026 — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- Units peaked at 1.81m in 2021 (lockdown peak), fell 2.5% in the latest year to a forecast 1.76m in 2023, and are forecast at 1.83m in 2026 — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- Share of domestic machine sales (2023): **pod 48.7%, espresso 27.2%, filter 24.1%**. Espresso is forecast to reach a 30% share by 2028, and pods are expected to remain the best-seller. Filter sales are forecast to rise by £5.72m to £44.9m over five years — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- The UK is about 9.8% of an estimated £1.65bn European consumer machine market and about 3.1% of a £5.23bn global market (2023) — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- 66% of UK pod machine owners bought their current model within the last three years (2023), which shows short replacement cycles — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- Allegra's Coffee At Home Report UK 2025 (published 9 Jun 2025, 216 pages, more than 2,000 consumer surveys) sizes the 2025 machine market by filter, espresso and pod. The figures are paywalled — [World Coffee Portal, 2025](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/industry-report/the-coffee-at-home-report-uk-2025/)
- Commissioned research-firm estimates (low reliability, and they conflict):
  - Grand View: UK coffee machine market about US$311.8m in 2025, 7.2% CAGR 2026–33. It says drip/filter is the largest segment and pod is the fastest-growing — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/coffee-machine-market/uk)
  - Transpire: US$319m in 2025, rising to US$511m by 2033 (about 6.1% CAGR) — [Transpire Insight](https://www.transpireinsight.com/report/united-kingdom-coffee-machine-market)
  - Statista: about US$0.34bn in 2024, about 4.3% CAGR 2024–29 (snippet only, with the detail paywalled) — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/household-appliances/small-appliances/coffee-machines/united-kingdom)
  - Verified Market Reports: UK bean-to-cup alone US$0.8bn (2024), rising to US$1.3bn by 2033. This conflicts with the totals above by about 3x and probably includes commercial machines — [Verified Market Reports](https://www.verifiedmarketreports.com/product/bean-to-cup-coffee-machine-market/)
- UK coffee pods (consumables) market about US$944.8m in 2026, 5.1% CAGR (Knowledge Sourcing Intelligence, cited secondhand) — [Coffee Nerds compilation](https://coffeenerds.co.uk/pages/how-the-uk-drinks-its-coffee-statistics)
- Supplier context (global): De'Longhi Group 2025 revenue about €3.8bn, +10.4% at constant currency. Coffee is about 65% of group revenue, with growth driven by manual/espresso machines and low-teens growth in coffee accessories. The professional division grew strongly (+40% in the division serving professional customers, Q3) — [Business Wire, Jan 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260128191504/en/De-Longhi-Group---Record-Preliminary-2025-Revenues-of-%E2%82%AC3.8-Billion-up-10.4-at-Constant-Exchange-Rates); [Stockopedia/Reuters](https://www.stockopedia.com/share-prices/de-longhi-sp-a-BIT:DLG/news/coffee-machines-drive-de-apos-longhi-apos-s-2025-profit-above-guidance-019e6a14-876f-72b6-8d3f-572fc1b68d88/)
- Ideal Home reported that the UK "discovered" bean-to-cup in 2025: its three most-clicked coffee machines of 2025 were all bean-to-cup (this is publisher traffic, not sales data) — [Ideal Home](https://www.idealhome.co.uk/all-rooms/coffee/most-clicked-coffee-machine-ih-readers-2025)
- Example 2025 Black Friday price points: De'Longhi Magnifica S bean-to-cup cut from £479.99 to £299 at Currys, and Siemens TQ515GB9 from £999 to £399 at John Lewis. This shows deep discounting in the £300–£1,000 bean-to-cup band — [Yours / roundup](https://www.yours.co.uk/life/home/black-friday-coffee-machine/); [T3](https://www.t3.com/home-living/coffee-machines/this-luxury-coffee-machine-has-had-a-huge-gbp600-price-drop-at-john-lewis)

### Inferences
- Implied average selling price is about £92 per unit (£162.8m ÷ 1.76m, 2023). The market is volume-led by £60–£150 pod and filter machines, while value growth comes from the £250–£1,000 bean-to-cup and espresso tier. Inferred price bands: pod £50–£200, filter £30–£150, manual espresso £100–£700 (Sage, De'Longhi), bean-to-cup £250–£1,500 (consumer), and commercial bean-to-cup £2,000–£6,000+ (see section 5).
- De'Longhi's strength in manual and accessories suggests the "home barista" tier (manual espresso plus grinder plus accessories) is the dynamic segment in 2025. The pod share is likely eroding slowly, but WCP still expected pods to lead.
- Bean-to-cup is not split out separately in the free WCP data. It is likely counted within "espresso".

### Gaps
- No free 2025 or 2026 UK value or unit data (Allegra 2025, GfK/NIQ and Mintel are all paywalled). No UK bean-to-cup unit numbers and no official ASP by segment.
- No UK brand shares from a reliable source. A LinkedIn claim of De'Longhi at 38% of bean-to-cup and Nespresso at 46% of pods is unverified ([Mintel search result context](https://www.mintel.com/insights/food-and-drink/home-coffee-trend/)).
- No UK commercial or office machine market size found.
- No Sage/Breville UK-specific results found.

## 2. At-home vs out-of-home spend, hybrid working, cost of living, and specialty adoption

### Takeaway
Out-of-home spending is not collapsing. The UK branded coffee shop market reached **£6.1bn (+5.2%) across 11,456 outlets** in the year to early 2025, and frequent visiting rose. At the same time, café prices (12oz latte about £3.51–£3.64, spend per visit £6.23) are pushing a parallel "café at home" trend. The main motivation is saving money (47%), and consumers estimate they save about £25 a month. Home and café use complement each other rather than one replacing the other.

### Cited Findings
- Branded coffee shop market £6.1bn, +5.2% year on year, 11,456 outlets, 570 net new outlets. Costa has 2,671 outlets, Greggs 2,610 and Starbucks 1,354. Industry leaders rate "price consciousness" as the number one consumer trend. The average 12oz latte costs £3.64 (Project Café UK 2025) — [World Coffee Portal, Jan 2025](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/value-in-the-spotlight-as-competition-heats-up-in-6-1bn-uk-branded-coffee-shop-market/)
- The share of consumers making multiple coffee shop visits per week rose from 56% to 60% — [World Coffee Portal, Project Café UK 2025 debrief](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/analysis/the-debrief-project-cafe-uk-2025/)
- Forecast: more than 13,200 outlets by January 2030 (3.0% five-year outlet CAGR) — [StoneX](https://www.stonex.com/en-gb/insights/new-report-shows-uk-branded-coffee-shop-sales-grew-5-2pct-1550760/)
- Average 12oz latte rose 8.7% to £3.51 (2024). Average spend per coffee shop visit is £6.23, +5% (WCP 2025). Average coffee costs £3.35, ranging from £2.69 to £3.72 by city (Finder 2025) — [Coffee Nerds compilation](https://coffeenerds.co.uk/pages/how-the-uk-drinks-its-coffee-statistics)
- Nespresso data (Oct 2026): consumers estimate they save £24.77 a month, almost £300 a year, by making coffee at home — [Retail Times, Oct 2026](https://retailtimes.co.uk/serving-coffee-at-home-is-saving-brits-nearly-300-a-year-nespresso-data-reveals/)
- Redrow/OnePulse (n=1,000, Jan 2026): 47% cite saving money as the main motivation for a home coffee station, and 73% still buy café coffee monthly. 78% say a coffee bar makes a home feel premium. Google searches for "kitchen coffee bar" are up 96% year on year — [KBN Weekly, Jul 2026](https://kbnweekly.co.uk/kitchen-coffee-bar-searches-up-96-as-home-cafe-trend-grows/)
- Censuswide (n=2,000): 56% of Brits own a coffee machine at home, and 38% use it daily (survey date not confirmed, reported via a search summary) — [search summary; original Censuswide release not fetched]
- Older Mintel ownership data (undated, probably pre-2020): 16% own an espresso or bean-to-cup machine, 14% a pod machine and 22% a filter machine. 30% would look for "coffee shop quality" when choosing a machine — [Mintel](https://www.mintel.com/insights/food-and-drink/home-coffee-trend/)
- 2023 WCP consumer survey: 81% drink at least one coffee at home daily and 57% drink several. Household purchase: instant 64%, ground 48%, pods 47% — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)
- About 98m cups a day (BCA, undated). 76% of UK adults regularly drink coffee (Statista 2025). 82% of households use coffee (Mintel 2025) — [Coffee Nerds compilation](https://coffeenerds.co.uk/pages/how-the-uk-drinks-its-coffee-statistics)
- Specialty and premium beans: direct-to-consumer 1kg bean bag sales rose 25% year on year at £12–£15/kg (Caffe Prima's own sales data, 2025, vendor source) — [Caffe Prima](https://www.caffeprima.co.uk/blogs/articles/uk-home-coffee-drinkers-show-preference-for-affordable-gourmet-beans)
- A home-brewed latte often costs less than £1, against £4–£5 in cafés. TikTok tutorials are cited as pushing home coffee mainstream (opinion piece, Dec 2025) — [Lawyer Monthly](https://www.lawyer-monthly.com/2025/12/why-uk-cant-get-enough-at-home-coffee/)
- 2023: 56% are more likely to buy coffee they know is environmentally friendly — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)

### Inferences
- Payback maths a new entrant can use: at about £3.50 a café latte against under £1 at home, a heavy user (5 a week) saves about £10–£13 a week. A £300–£500 bean-to-cup machine therefore pays back in roughly 6–12 months. This is the core of "the machine pays for itself" messaging.
- Hybrid working: no direct UK data was found. The logic is that 2–3 home-working days a week move the weekday coffee into the home, while offices need fewer but better machines (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025 or 2026 total UK at-home coffee spend in £ (an uncited "£6+ a week per household" figure appears on a vendor blog, [Mini & Mighty](https://www.miniandmightyespresso.co.uk/2025/09/17/uk-coffee-market-2025/)).
- No hard data on the hybrid-working effect.
- No current specialty-coffee penetration figure for the UK.

## 3. Buyer personas, social research journey, seasonality, and returns

### Takeaway
Younger buyers are heavily social-led: 79% of Gen Z and 73% of millennials have tried viral coffee recipes, against 21% of Boomers, and 68% of Gen Z make iced or cold coffee at home. Older adults (50+) are the heaviest daily drinkers. Machines are strongly promoted around Black Friday and Christmas gifting, with instalment pricing. No hard UK data was found on research channels or return rates.

### Cited Findings
- Tried viral coffee recipes: Gen Z 79%, millennials 73%, Boomers 21%. 68% of Gen Z drink iced or cold brew at home and 22% drink iced coffee year-round. 19% of millennials have tried functional coffee (collagen or mushroom). 51% say the coffee they serve guests matters, and 27% try to recreate a café experience when hosting (Nespresso, Oct 2026) — [Retail Times](https://retailtimes.co.uk/serving-coffee-at-home-is-saving-brits-nearly-300-a-year-nespresso-data-reveals/)
- Gen Z's favourite coffee is the latte, while older generations default to instant (Statista 2025). 46% of Gen Z never drink coffee at home or work (YouGov, year not stated). Adults aged 50+ are the most likely to drink several cups a day — [Coffee Nerds compilation](https://coffeenerds.co.uk/pages/how-the-uk-drinks-its-coffee-statistics)
- Black Friday: Currys, John Lewis, Argos, AO, Appliances Direct and Amazon are the main channels. Deep discounts are common (about 35–60% off headline RRP on bean-to-cup). John Lewis shows instalment pricing, for example a Siemens machine from £38.38 a month at 24.9% APR, with John Lewis acting as credit broker — [Yours](https://www.yours.co.uk/life/home/black-friday-coffee-machine/); [John Lewis Black Friday coffee machines](https://www.johnlewis.com/browse/black-friday/view-all-coffee-machines/_/N-5nmdZ4747)
- Up to £700 off De'Longhi espresso machines at Black Friday 2025 — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/coffee-machines/yes-you-really-can-get-up-to-gbp700-off-delonghi-espresso-machines-for-black-friday-heres-how)
- TikTok Shop is a growth channel for UK coffee brands. Grind, for example, grew via TikTok Shop and earned social in 2025 — [Corq Studio](https://corq.studio/insights/coffee-tea-and-matcha-how-grind-perfectted-and-twinings-drove-growth-via-tiktok-shop-and-earned-social-in-2025/)
- Selfridges: coffee machine sales up 20% year on year, with Lavazza up 85%, and it is selling more espresso cups than mugs (date unconfirmed) — [Housewares Live](https://housewareslive.net/selfridges-selling-more-espresso-cups-than-mugs/)
- Comparison and review media are a key research step. Which? runs best-buy and "don't buy" lists, and Ideal Home, T3, TechRadar and the Guardian publish bean-to-cup reviews — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/coffee-machines/article/best-bean-to-cup-coffee-machines); [Ideal Home](https://www.idealhome.co.uk/all-rooms/coffee/most-clicked-coffee-machine-ih-readers-2025)

### Inferences
- Likely personas:
  1. Gen Z and millennial "home café" or iced-latte makers, socially led (TikTok/Instagram), who want milk drinks and aesthetics.
  2. Hybrid-working 30–50s saving on the daily café habit, who favour bean-to-cup convenience.
  3. 50+ heavy drinkers trading up from instant or filter.
  4. Enthusiasts (the r/espresso and YouTube "James Hoffmann" audience) buying manual espresso plus a grinder.
  5. Gift buyers in Q4 who are price- and deal-driven.
- Q4 (Black Friday through Christmas) likely concentrates a large share of annual unit sales, but no UK figure was found.

### Gaps
- No UK data on research-journey channel shares (YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, comparison sites).
- No coffee-machine return rate data.
- No share of sales that are gifts, and no Q4 seasonality percentage.

## 4. Pain points and complaints

### Takeaway
Which?'s 2025 survey of 5,864 owners shows faults cluster around **dispensing problems (20% of faulty machines), leaks (19%) and cracked bodies (8%)**. Mineral build-up and inadequate descaling are the leading causes of poor performance. Repair and parts support varies sharply by brand: Sage offers no out-of-warranty repairs, Lavazza no repair service at all, while Krups and Bosch offer 10–15 years of parts. Pods carry a heavy waste burden, with recycling rates only about 21–35%.

### Cited Findings
- Which? brand reliability survey: 5,864 owners, April 2025. The most common faults (from more than 4,000 owners) are dispensing 20% (blockages, sometimes from too fine a grind), leaks 19% (over-tamping or a full drip tray) and cracked or damaged body 8%. Brand-level scores are paywalled — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/coffee-machines/article/top-coffee-machine-brands-aHDL51d47Bti)
- After-sales service differences — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/coffee-machines/article/top-coffee-machine-brands-aHDL51d47Bti):
  - Sage: 2-year guarantee, no repairs after it, parts sold online.
  - De'Longhi: out-of-warranty repairs, but limited spare parts online.
  - Lavazza: 12-month warranty, with no repair service or online parts.
  - Krups: parts for up to 15 years.
  - Bosch: 2-year warranty (5 if registered), parts for 10 years.
  - Lakeland: 3-year standard guarantee.
- Descaling: mineral build-up is the leading cause of poor performance. Using the wrong descaler can damage machines and void warranties — [Which? (via search summary)](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/coffee-machines/article/top-coffee-machine-brands-aHDL51d47Bti)
- Anecdotal (US-focused) complaint lists for Nespresso and Keurig cite descaling-triggered failures and user neglect of descaling — [The Daily Meal](https://www.thedailymeal.com/2240011/common-customer-complaints-nespresso-machines/); [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/2250932/common-customer-complaints-keurig-coffee-machines/)
- Pod waste:
  - Nespresso's UK recycling facilitation is about 21% (HuffPost UK, undated).
  - Global rates are cited at 28%, 32% and 35% across sources, against a 60% target by 2030.
  - An estimated 300,000 pods go to UK landfill each year (Expert Reviews; this figure looks low and is unverified).
  - Podback (launched April 2021) funds kerbside collection.
  - Sources: [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/coffee-machines/how-are-nespresso-pods-recycled); [Just Drinks](https://www.just-drinks.com/features/coffee-pods-an-environmental-menace-or-an-opportunity-for-sustainable-innovation); [Expert Reviews](https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/coffee-machines/are-coffee-pods-bad-for-the-environment); [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/podback-pioneers-a-new-way-to-recycle/669025.article)
- Industry insiders (2023): 51% say coffee retailers are not environmentally friendly enough, and 36% say machine makers could do more — [World Coffee Portal, 2023](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/news/uk-at-home-coffee-market-set-for-growth-as-consumers-bring-cafe-quality-to-their-kitchens/)

### Inferences
- Opportunities from pain points:
  1. Bundled maintenance: auto-ship descaler and filters, with water-hardness setup by postcode, since the UK has many hard-water areas.
  2. Out-of-warranty repair and servicing, a gap left by Sage and Lavazza.
  3. Guided onboarding for grind and dial-in, since "too fine a grind" causes dispensing faults.
  4. Messaging on bean cost per cup against pods: beans at £12–£15/kg is about 15–25p per shot, against about 40–80p for pods (inferred calculation, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No systematic Trustpilot or Reddit theme analysis was retrievable in this pass. The pain points above come from Which? and press. Complexity, choice overload and cleaning burden are plausible but unquantified for the UK.

## 5. Small-business and SME segment

### Takeaway
UK SMEs mostly acquire bean-to-cup machines through **3–5 year lease or rental**, typically **£10–£25 a week** (£40–£110 a month) ex-VAT for small-office machines. Larger espresso or bean-to-cup units for higher volumes cost up to about £180 a month or more. Pricing is driven by drinks per day (about 50 a day for compact machines, against 200–300 for high-capacity ones), fresh versus powdered milk, and lease term. No data was found on the sector mix (salons, gyms, Airbnb).

### Cited Findings
- Caffia: a £2,000 + VAT machine costs about £80 + VAT a month on rental. A £3,000 package costs £99–£114 + VAT a month on a 3-year lease. A JURA W8 costs £16.57 a week (3-year) or £10.59 a week (5-year). Bean-to-cup weekly rates range from about £10.60 to £95.51 — [Caffia rental](https://caffia.com/coffee-machine-rental); [Caffia cost guide](https://caffia.com/blog/how-much-does-a-commercial-coffee-machine-cost-in-the-uk/)
- Jurang: Coffetek Vitro S1 EVO from £69.37 a month, Vitro Series 3 Espresso from £180.51 a month, Bestir BM30 from £86.45 a month — [Jurang](https://www.jurang.co.uk/shop/220-coffetek-bean-to-cup-machines)
- Liquidline leases from £12.50 a week, and Iron & Fire leases a Cimbali S15 from £24.50 a week — [Liquidline](https://www.liquidline.co.uk/news/the-best-office-coffee-machines/); [Iron & Fire](https://wholesale.ironandfire.co.uk/commercial-coffee-machines/bean-to-cup/)
- Sizing: compact professional machines suit about 50 drinks a day, and high-capacity machines about 200–300 a day — [Caffia small office](https://caffia.com/small-office-coffee-machines/)
- De'Longhi's professional division grew strongly in 2025, including +40% revenue in the professional-customer division in Q3. This suggests rising demand for commercial-grade machines (global figure) — [Stockopedia/Reuters](https://www.stockopedia.com/share-prices/de-longhi-sp-a-BIT:DLG/news/coffee-machines-drive-de-apos-longhi-apos-s-second-outlook-hike-of-the-year-updated-019e69f5-711d-79bb-a1d0-c6aa22a114fb/)

### Inferences
- Micro-businesses (salons, estate agents, car showrooms, Airbnb hosts) often fall between two models. Consumer bean-to-cup machines (£300–£800) are not rated for commercial use, so warranties may be void. Commercial leases (£50–£100+ a month on 3–5 year terms, often with credit checks) are overkill for 10–30 cups a day.
- A short-term, all-inclusive "machine plus beans plus service" subscription for 10–50 cups a day is a plausible white space (inference).
- "Free on loan" models (machine free in exchange for a minimum ingredient or bean purchase) are common in the trade, but no source was retrieved in this pass.

### Gaps
- No UK office coffee or SME machine market size.
- No data by vertical, no lease against buy against free-on-loan split, and no average SME spend.

## 6. Ownership models: subscriptions, finance/BNPL, refurbished

### Takeaway
Retailers routinely offer credit instalments (for example John Lewis at 24.9% APR). Refurbished channels (Back Market, eBay, brand outlets) actively promote Sage and other machines as Black Friday alternatives. No quantified UK data was found on BNPL penetration, subscription uptake or refurbished market size.

### Cited Findings
- John Lewis instalment pricing on coffee machines (for example £38.38 a month at 24.9% APR), as credit broker — [John Lewis](https://www.johnlewis.com/browse/black-friday/view-all-coffee-machines/_/N-5nmdZ4747)
- Refurbished Sage machines on Back Market are graded "excellent" or "good" and are promoted as cheaper than Black Friday deals on new machines. eBay advertises up to 50% off refurbished tech — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/home/coffee-machines/skip-the-black-friday-sales-and-grab-one-of-these-refurbished-sage-coffee-makers-instead)
- Bean subscriptions are growing: Pact Coffee reports double-digit growth (cited in the Allegra 2025 report description) — [World Coffee Portal](https://www.worldcoffeeportal.com/industry-report/the-coffee-at-home-report-uk-2025/)

### Inferences
- Short replacement cycles (66% of pod owners bought within 3 years, 2023) and high bean-to-cup RRPs with frequent large discounts suggest room for a credible refurbished or trade-in programme and for "machine-as-a-service" home subscriptions. Validation is needed.

### Gaps
- No data on UK coffee machine BNPL (Klarna or PayPal Credit) usage.
- No refurbished or second-hand market size.
- No machine-subscription adoption rates.
